Pick a look for the Evrakçı window

Five directions, each drawn as the same Kontrol screen with the same invented data. The cut types are a QR page, a "her 3 sayfa" filter and a manual cut. Belge 41 needs a check, and pages 214–218 are selected. Click an image to open it at full size.

1Kartotek drawn by the skill's roll

An office card-index cabinet. Each document begins with a manila guide tab sticking up above its pages, and the tab's position tells how it was cut: left for QR, centre for filter, right for manual. Documents that need a check get an orange clip.

Good at
Seeing where documents start, and the heading names the span on screen ("Belge 40 — Belge 43"). A table of values sits right under the pages.
Risk
It can feel a bit "stationery" if the paper look goes too far.

2Hat Haritası my pick

Metro wayfinding signs. The whole job is one line along the top with a tick per document and a yellow diamond for each check. Below it are lanes for documents, pages, templates and values, lined up like a video editor.

Good at
Never losing your place in 800 pages, with very clear cut markers (filled disc, dashed ring, scissors square).
Risk
The most familiar of the five; it's the draft you were sent before.

3Terminal Sarısı

Airport signs. A yellow band under the tabs always says what to do next: "Sıradaki kontrol: Belge 41 · Sicil_No emin değil", with "6 kontrol kaldı" and a Sonraki (N) button. Document numbers are large, like gate numbers.

Good at
Working through the checks one by one; it's hard to miss anything.
Risk
Strong yellow; it only works if yellow means "next check" and nothing else.

4Pul Katalog

A stamp catalogue. About 60 small pages fit on screen at once, numbered and grouped by document, with documents that need a check circled in red pen. A slider makes the stamps bigger for weaker eyes.

Good at
Scanning a big pile fast and spotting odd splits at a glance.
Risk
Loses the left-to-right timeline feel, and is dense for long sessions.

5Windows standardı the familiar option

A clean Windows 11 office app: a left menu instead of top tabs, white cards, blue selection and an amber warning style.

Good at
Looking like every other Windows program staff already use; nothing to learn.
Risk
No character of its own.

How to choose

Reply in the chat with a number, or a mix such as "3, with the timeline from 2". You can also ask for a fresh set, safer or bolder. Once you pick, all six screens (Yükle, Kontrol, Şablonlar, Değerler, Dışa Aktar, Rehber) get drawn in that look for your approval before anything is built.
