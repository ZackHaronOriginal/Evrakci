## Direction contract

THESIS: Every document is a card in an index drawer; cuts are guide cards whose tabs rise above the pile. It refuses the grey file-manager grid and the dashboard of boxed panels.

OWN-WORLD: Office card-index cabinet. Cabinet green-grey shell (header, tabs, status bar) with white lettering; index-card white sheets on a pale grey-green ground; manila guide tabs; a thin red index-card rule under headings; orange metal signal riders mean "kontrol" (always with icon and text). Cut type = tab position and pictogram (left QR, centre filter with dashed edge, right manual scissors). Humanist sans at a large base, tabular figures. Groups separate by type and thin rules, not boxes.

STORY: Staff see where each document starts and why, see which ones carry a rider, fix them by keyboard, export, and trust nothing was silent.
