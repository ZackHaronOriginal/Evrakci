# Dışa Aktar audit: click paths

Phase 4, Görev A, screen Dışa Aktar. Every click, double click, right click, drag, wheel and key on the elements of `disa-aktar-inventory.md` that could be driven, plus the common mistakes (double click on a button, a drag to the wrong place, cancel in the middle, close or resize during a task, an export into a full, read-only or missing folder), the long-content and many-file cases, the delete-after-export retention case (B-008), Ctrl+P (B-047) and the QAction.triggered default-parameter pattern.

Result: **247 paths tried, 195 pass, 51 fail, 1 not decidable**. Rows with a finding id and result "pass" are findings found by looking at what the passing path did (the Note column says what). Several failing rows share one finding: 20 distinct findings come from the failing rows (01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 22, 25, 26).

How the rows were made: offscreen (`QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen`), synthetic data only (`tools/screenshots.py:build` pattern with 3 to 400 invented documents, QR separator pages, two or three template variables), a window built fresh per scenario in light 100 % at 1366x768 unless the row says otherwise, driven with QTest (mouse, keys) and direct calls on `DisaAktarScreen`. The real `export_job` ran for every export (real tesseract for the text-layer rows: 3 documents in 1 s); a gate inside a wrapper of `export_job` held a run between two files so that cancel, close, resize, F5, Ctrl+Z and tab switches could be tried mid-export. The native dialogs (folder, print), `open_path` and `reveal_path` were replaced by stubs that record the call. Read-only and unlistable folders were made by running the same script as the unprivileged user `nobody` (the sandbox user is root, for whom `os.access` is always true). Scripts live in the session scratchpad (`da/`), not in the repository; screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/p4/disa-aktar/states/`.

Rules of the table: "Expected" is what the product documents (PRODUCT.md, the research inventory, the tooltip or accessible name of the element) or, where nothing is documented, "records behaviour" in the Note column. Pass means actual matches expected. Offscreen limits: no OS drag loop, no native dialog, no taskbar, no real Menu key (a keyboard `QContextMenuEvent` was sent instead).

Already in the ledger and seen again: B-008 (job stays open after "Tam dışa aktarınca sil"; reproduced and extended: F-disa-aktar-03), B-047 (Ctrl+P; verified fixed: the three ways in and `triggered(True)` on all 10 actions of the tab), B-009 (every window export test unchecks the text layer; here one real default-OCR run passed: P-139).
