## Questions the run lead asked, answered

1. **AUD-WIN-02, the guide reader's scroll bar: is scrolling a long guide legitimate? Yes. It is a rule problem, not a product bug (F-rehber-16).** The rule says "no window-sized scroll area shows a scroll bar (long lists may scroll)"; its sources, PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md Layout, mean that the window frame and the controls must fit 1366x768 and that panes scroll inside their own area. The Rehber reader is a card inside the window, not the window: it is a reading pane with a thin 9 px bar, the same as the Kontrol timeline or a table. It fails only because the check exempts `QAbstractItemView` and not a text reader. The guide cannot fit by design: 1,693 px of text at 100 % (2.7 screens of the 622 px reader at 1366x768, 1.8 screens at 1920x1080 because the column is capped at 680 px), 2,406 px at 150 % text (4.0 screens). Shrinking it to fit would break the 13 px type floor or cut the guide. The count is 9 failing combinations for Rehber (the "12" is the whole app: 9 Rehber, 3 Şablonlar, F-sablonlar-23). The rule is also not measuring "needs a scroll bar": at 1366x768 150 % the same reader shows a bar and the rule passes, only because the reader's area is 48 % of the window, under the half-window threshold. Proposal for the owner (Q-03): exempt document readers and fitted canvases like item views, or measure the window's own scroll, and keep the rule for real layout overflow. Reading itself works: wheel, PgUp/PgDn, Home/End, arrows, Space, the contents list and the status line "Bölüm n / 7" all agree (P-007 to P-014).
2. **Search, F3 and Shift+F3 with no hits (F-rehber-17, S4).** With hits they work (next, previous, wrap round, from the reader, the field and the list, not from another tab: P-084 to P-095). With no hits the two keys and the chevron buttons are disabled and silent: nothing moves, nothing is said. The "Sonuç yok" text of `step()` is only an accessible name that a screen reader may announce when Enter is pressed in the field; it is never drawn and F3 never reaches it. A sighted user sees the "no result" card (the answer is on the screen already), so this is polish; a screen-reader user hears something only after Enter. With an empty field F3 does nothing, which is acceptable.
3. **Printing separator sheets, B-007 page versus sheet wording (F-rehber-03, S3).** Confirmed and wider than the ledger row: the number box says "Kaç adet [20] sayfa", its tooltip says "ayıraç sayfası sayısı", the toast says "20 ayıraç sayfası (40 sayfa) yazıcıya gönderildi" (so "sayfa" means a sheet in the box and a page in the brackets), the guide says "yaprağın iki yüzüne", the CLI says "yaprak". Two things are new: the print job never asks for two-sided output (duplex is not set, the default is one-sided), so unless the person ticks it in the system dialog, 20 separators take 40 sheets of paper; and the printed sheet itself carries missing-glyph boxes in its two labels (F-rehber-02).
4. **The blank-pages promise (F-rehber-04, S3; ledger B-006).** Confirmed. The guide says blank back sides "iş ayarından atabilirsiniz"; no control, menu entry, palette command or tooltip in the six tabs mentions blank pages (checked by walking every visible widget and the whole action registry). Only `evrakci is bos-sayfalar ac` sets it. New evidence: the demo job keeps its blank page inside document 2 (pages per document 3 / 7 / 3); with the setting on it is 3 / 6 / 3, so by default a blank back side goes into the exported PDF and the guide does not say so. "Boş sayfalar hiçbir zaman belge ayırmaz" is true (P-232).
5. **Long content at 150 %.** The shipped guide is fine at 150 % and 1366x768 (S:rehber matrix image, 0 clipping lines): the reader is 839 x 598 px and shows 4 screens of text; the print card lies below the fold of the side panel by design (DESIGN.md Layout) and is reached by wheel, Tab and Ctrl+P. Content longer than the shipped guide breaks two things: a guide of 10 or more sections at 150 % (12 at 100 %) makes the contents card taller than the panel, so the card scroller hides it (F-rehber-11); long section titles are cut with "…" and no tooltip (F-rehber-22). A 160-character unbroken word wraps inside the card and nothing is cut (P-204). The guide's 30-section test (S:long-30-sections-light-1366x768-150) shows the blank panel.

## Not findings, noted

- Keyboard: the whole tab is reachable (Ctrl+6 puts focus in the reader, Tab visits 18 named stops, F6 cycles the regions, Ctrl+F reaches the field and shows the side panel after F9, Esc clears). The "no result" buttons are reachable too, 18 Tab presses from the search field (the first 10 presses do not reach them, the main pane comes before the side panel in the walk).
- Search matching: Turkish-aware (AYIRAC, ayirac, İKİ/iki all match), plain substring, regex characters and HTML are taken literally, 258 hits mark in 16 ms (P-106 to P-129). Phrases across paragraphs are not found by design.
- Printing: 1 sheet and 200 sheets both write in under a second to a PDF printer (the GUI thread is busy that long; a real spooler was not measured). The QR sits 68 mm from the sides and 80 mm from the top, so a printer's unprintable edge cannot touch it (answers inventory observation 8).
- A PDF dropped on the guide is taken by the window as a scan for the open job (15 -> 16 pages, tab jumps to Yükle), as on any tab; not a Rehber finding.
