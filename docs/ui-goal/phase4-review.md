# Phase 4 review: findings and what was done

Reviewers: **visual-office** (screenshots against the approved direction A PNGs), **a11y-keyboard** (keyboard, WCAG, copy), **code** (the Phase 3 diff). Every high and medium finding was fixed or, where the fix would change A's approved look, built as far as A allows and logged for the owner in `mockups/final-a/PROPOSALS.md` (section "Phase 4 review"). Cheap lows were fixed too.

Verification after the last group of fixes: `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen python3 -m pytest -q` gives 1075 passed, 6 skipped (Phase 3 ended at 1031 passed). `ruff check .` is clean. The new tests are in `tests/ui/test_phase4.py`, plus additions in `test_yukle_a.py`, `test_shell.py`, `test_tokens.py`, `test_kontrol_a.py` and `test_degerler_model.py`. The screenshot matrix was shot again into `docs/ui-goal/shots/phase4/`, with the custom title band and its caption buttons on every platform. A's other states are in `shots/phase4/states/`.

Owner decision taken in during this phase: decisions.md, "Owner answer: tabs at 150 % text", option **b** (a "»" overflow menu). It is built and replaces the icon-only rule.
