## What we do today (short)

- Values carry five levels: Emin, Orta, Kontrol, Okunamadı, Okunmadı (V-27 to V-31). Review views: Kontrol gerekenler, İncelenmemiş, Tümü, Örnek (V-37 to V-40, V-67, V-68).
- Enter = "Onayla ve sonrakine geç"; F2 types by hand; N / Shift+N jump to the next value that needs a check (V-51, V-57, V-59). Every change is one undo step (V-64).
- A random sample of up to 20 confident values is drawn for spot-checking; one error found marks the other unreviewed values of that kind (V-68, V-69, V-70).
- Kontrol marks documents with an orange "Kontrol" rider and tells the reason in a sentence (K-54, K-55, K-57); N / Shift+N jumps between them (K-65); U jumps to the next not-approved document (K-66).

## Ideas for the owner's ranking

1. **Show which cell is wrong.** Problem: row-level look hides the one bad cell among four (O-01). Who gains: every reviewer, long sessions. Evidence: [Transact docs](https://ephesoft.com/docs/?p=47801) mark status at field-group and document level. Effort: M.
2. **Calibration line for the sample.** Problem: nobody learns whether Emin at 0.90 is trustworthy. Show "N errors in M sampled Emin values" per template after a sample and let the owner see it over time. Who gains: the owner and the person who sets templates. Evidence: [ACL NLP4DH 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.nlp4dh-1.20.pdf), [arXiv 0907.0418](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0418). Effort: S.
3. **Scale the sample with the batch and add a stop rule.** Problem: 20 values for 800 documents; no rule for "too many errors, re-read". Evidence: [NARA guide](https://Archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/policy/digitization-quality-mgmt-guide.pdf) (analogy, not the same task). Effort: S.
4. **Show every section's reading next to the suggestion** (O-16), so Enter does not rest on one anchor. Evidence: [PMC5356416](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5356416) (verification is the mitigation). Effort: S.
5. **Same variable across documents: crop strip.** Problem: systematic box shift is hard to see one document at a time. Evidence: [ABBYY docs](https://docs.abbyy.com/flexi-capture/operator-work/field-verification-operator-work). Effort: M.
6. **Carry the document from Değerler to Kontrol (fix O-09).** Effort: S.
