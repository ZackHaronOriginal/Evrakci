Exact versions of every bundled Python package and their licence files:
  licenses\VERSIONS.txt and licenses\python\<package>\

Qt / PySide6 source (LGPL): the exact PySide6 version is listed in VERSIONS.txt. Its source is
available from https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/ and https://pypi.org/project/PySide6/
(source distribution), and on written request from the Evrakçı maintainer for at least three years.
To use your own Qt build, replace the files in lib\PySide6\ with a compatible build.

Ghostscript, PyMuPDF and PyQt are not used and not included.
