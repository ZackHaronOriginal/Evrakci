Linux build
-----------
Paths above are written the Windows way; on Linux the same files are under lib/ and
licenses/ with forward slashes. The Tesseract program is not bundled on Linux (it comes from
the system package, Apache-2.0); its small output config files (configs/, tessconfigs/,
Apache-2.0) are bundled in tessdata_best/. The rows for tesseract.exe and its DLLs apply to
Windows only.

Libraries of the Python runtime, in lib/ (licence and copyright texts in licenses/linux/):
  OpenSSL 3 (libssl, libcrypto)   Apache-2.0 (Apache-2.0.txt)
  libffi                          MIT (libffi.txt)
  bzip2 (libbz2)                  bzip2 licence, BSD-style (bzip2.txt)
  xz (liblzma)                    0BSD / public domain (xz.txt; its GPL parts are the xz tools,
                                  which are not included)
  mpdecimal (libmpdec)            BSD-2-Clause (mpdecimal.txt)
  libuuid (util-linux)            BSD-3-Clause (libuuid.txt; only libuuid is included)
ICU (libicu*, in lib/PySide6/Qt/lib/), used by Qt: Unicode licence (ICU.txt)

Left out on purpose (GPL-only): GNU Readline, GDBM, Qt Virtual Keyboard and Qt Wayland
Compositor. Both builds remove those Qt parts, and the build fails if any of them remains.

Exact versions of every bundled Python package and their licence files:
  licenses\VERSIONS.txt and licenses\python\<package>\

Qt / PySide6 source (LGPL): the exact PySide6 version is listed in VERSIONS.txt. Its source is
available from https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/ and https://pypi.org/project/PySide6/
(source distribution), and on written request from the Evrakçı maintainer for at least three years.
To use your own Qt build, replace the files in lib\PySide6\ with a compatible build.

Ghostscript, PyMuPDF and PyQt are not used and not included.
